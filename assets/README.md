# Clip farm assets

Drop your audio + B-roll here (gitignored — keep them out of the repo):

- `love_me.mp3` — the song. Use a ROYALTY-FREE "Love Me" soundalike for Devpost
  uploads (the real track is copyrighted); real trending audio only on personal
  social posts. Any of .mp3/.m4a/.wav/.ogg works; first match wins.
- `broll/` — images or short videos flashed during the song drop, Superman-edit
  style (heroic/selfless moments). .jpg/.png/.mp4/.mov/.webm; shown in filename
  order, cycled to fill the drop. Prefix with numbers (01_, 02_) to order them.
